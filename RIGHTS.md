# Rights and removal

## Whose work is what

- **Prompts, videos and images** in this catalog come from public posts. They belong to their authors. Every entry credits the author, links to their profile and to the original post. We do not relicense them, and the licence in [LICENSE](LICENSE) does not cover them.
- **Editorial text written by Wandy3D**: the titles, summaries, "how to use" steps and the build briefs labelled "Build brief by Wandy3D". This text is licensed under [CC BY 4.0](LICENSE).
- **Wandy3D originals** (author "Wandy3D") are our own prompts and videos.
- Product and model names (Claude, GPT, Kimi, Three.js, Blender …) belong to their owners. A listing here does not imply endorsement.

## Are you the author?

You can ask us to edit, re-credit or remove an entry, on GitHub and on wandy3d.com:

- open a [removal request](https://github.com/wornoxgames/awesome-3d-prompts/issues/new?template=removal.md), or
- e-mail **support@wandy3d.com** with the link to your post.

We hide the page on wandy3d.com and remove the entry here as soon as we have checked the request. We usually do this within a few days. You don't need to give a reason.
